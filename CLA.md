# Freeman Notes Contributor Licence Agreement

**Version 1.0**

> **Draft pending legal review.** This agreement has not yet been reviewed by counsel. It is
> published so contributors can read it, and will be finalised before it is enforced.

Thank you for contributing to Freeman Notes. This agreement sets out the terms on which your
contribution is accepted. It is deliberately short, and this box says what it actually does:

- **You keep the copyright in your work.** This is a licence, not an assignment. Nothing is
  transferred away from you, and you may use your own contribution however you like, elsewhere,
  forever.
- **You grant the Owner broad rights**, including the right to release your contribution under
  licences other than the AGPL.
- **Why that last part is needed:** Freeman Notes is AGPL-3.0. Some organisations cannot accept
  copyleft terms, and the Owner offers them a commercial licence instead. That is only possible if
  the Owner holds sufficient rights in *all* of the code, including yours. Without this agreement,
  a single contribution would remove that option permanently.

Signing is a one-time step. A bot will comment on your first pull request with a link.

## How to sign

Open a pull request. A bot will comment with a link to this document and ask you to reply with a
single line confirming that you have read and agree to it. Your signature is recorded against your
GitHub account so you only ever do this once.

If you would rather not sign, that is completely fine — please still open issues, report bugs and
suggest ideas. Those need no agreement of any kind.
